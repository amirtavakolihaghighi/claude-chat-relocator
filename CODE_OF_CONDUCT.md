# Code of Conduct

## The short version

Be decent to people. Assume good faith. Criticise code, not the person who
wrote it.

## What is expected

- Be welcoming to people regardless of experience, background, nationality,
  language, gender, or anything else that is not the work in front of you.
- Give feedback on the work. "This will corrupt files when the path has a
  trailing slash" is useful. "This is amateur nonsense" is not.
- Accept feedback the same way. Someone pointing out a flaw in your patch is
  doing you a favour.
- Remember that most people here are writing in a second language, and in their
  spare time.

## What is not acceptable

- Harassment, insults, or personal attacks of any kind.
- Demeaning comments about someone's identity or background.
- Publishing anyone's private information.
- Deliberately wasting maintainers' or contributors' time.

## Scope

This applies in issues, pull requests, discussions, commit messages, and
anywhere else this project is represented.

## Reporting

If someone is behaving this way, open an issue saying only that you would like
to make private contact, or use GitHub's report function. Reports are handled
privately and in confidence.

Maintainers may edit, hide, or delete comments, and may block accounts, when
this document is not being respected.
